# Worked example — JD-mirroring in practice

A fully de-identified walkthrough of the tailoring step. Every employer, candidate and
project here is generic on purpose.

## The posting (excerpt)

> **Channel Marketing Specialist (Graduate)**
>
> Responsibilities:
> 1. New-product marketing: support launch planning and execution — brief the copy,
>    prepare materials, coordinate suppliers, and ensure launches ship on schedule.
> 2. Channel support: help open and maintain online and offline marketing channels;
>    prepare channel materials, follow up on stock placement, and collect and
>    summarise sales data to keep channel operations on pace.
>
> Requirements:
> - Bachelor's degree or above; CET-6.
> - Strong copywriting, event-execution and cross-departmental communication skills.
> - Familiar with office software.
> - Fast learner, resilient, accountable, collaborative.
>
> Nice to have:
> - Experience in a student organisation, club, or campus marketing activity (leader preferred).
> - Familiar with design and video-editing tools.
> - Social-media content experience.

## Step 2 — Evidence bank (extract from the candidate's history)

| id | Evidence, as facts |

## Step 4 — Write the bullets

**Weak (before):**

> - Participated in campus activity planning and execution, improving communication skills.
> - Responsible for data collection and analysis, developing data sensitivity.

**Strong (after):**

> - Co-owned a campus careers event end-to-end: drafted the promotional copy, produced
>   visual assets with design and video tools, coordinated speakers and registrants, and
>   tracked sign-ups — the event shipped on schedule and reached the target student cohort,
>   building hands-on launch-execution and material-preparation skills.
> - Compiled and reviewed the event's registration data, then ran a retrospective on what to
>   repeat — forming a plan → execute → collect → improve loop directly transferable to
>   channel-operations reporting.

Notice how the requirement's own nouns (launch, copy, materials, on schedule, channel
operations, data) reappear, while every claim still traces to E1.

## Step 5 — Assemble the header keyword line

Chosen from the matched requirements only:

> `文案撰写 ｜ 活动执行 ｜ 物料筹备（设计/剪辑）｜ 跨部门沟通`

R1's "supplier coordination" and R3's "sales data" are not claimed at specialist level,
so they do not appear in the keyword line — they surface in the bullet body where the
partial match is visible and honest.

## The tailoring log

```
Version tag:   grad-channel-marketing-v1
Emphasised:    R4, R5, R6, R7, R8
Partial:       R1, R2, R3   (campus / research scale, stated honestly)
Unaddressed:   none
Left out:      unrelated coursework detail (frees space for R1-R3)
```

Store this log next to the built document. When an interview asks about the resume, this
is the sheet that tells you what you claimed and why.
